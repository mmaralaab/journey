Handmade Web - J.R. Carpender

Reading A Handmade Web made me realize how little I think about the internet as something I can actually create myself. I'm so used to going on Instagram, TikTok, or other platforms that I never really question who controls the space I'm using. I found Carpenter's idea of the handmade web interesting because it reminded me that the internet can also be personal and experimental. The idea that websites don't always need to be perfect or follow the same design standards makes me feel less overwhelmed about creating my own website. I like the idea of something feeling handmade and personal because you can see the person behind it. It makes me feel like I don't have to make my website perfect, but instead can let it reflect my own personality and creativity. It can always grow over time.
